Appearance reference and specification boundaries
21 October 2026

   The locked device image: front champagne bezel, plain dark oval face,

These names identify reference assets in the website project, not files
necessarily included in this CAD package. They guide appearance; they are not
orthographic drawings or measured dimensional evidence. The older, unmounted
TechnologyTour imagery with a circular brushed face was not used as the target.

CHANGES MADE TO THE MODEL
- Replaced the branded front treatment with a plain crowned satin-black insert.
- Moved the champagne accent to a visible chamfered bezel around that insert.
- Tapered the body behind the bezel and introduced restrained smoked-shell
  transmission in the viewer.
- Reshaped the retention fin into a rounded, deeply curled horn. Its separate
  black conductive-silicone active contact follows the actual curved surface.
- Enlarged the five nominal tips to 12 / 13.2 / 14.4 / 15.6 / 16.8 mm, used
  smoke-grey silicone, exposed more neck, and narrowed the two EEG inlays.
- Slimmed the hub to a nominal 25 × 77 × 12 mm and reduced the trunk to 3 mm OD.
- Added curve-following braid shading, retaining the actual full-length cable
  solids, and posed the Product pair to combine the locked reference’s exterior/contact
  presentation with the active hero’s inward-facing tips.
- The assembled Product view shows leads derived from the first 80 mm of each
  branch’s sampled centreline; only the first 22 mm contributes to framing,
  allowing the rest to crop naturally at the stage edge. These are temporary
  presentation meshes parented to the posed earbuds, excluded from part counts
  and CAD exports, and hidden in exploded views. Full cables remain in System.

DELIBERATE DIFFERENCES REQUIRED BY SPECIFICATION v0.3
- The locked image shows multiple hub controls. This model retains ONE physical
  stop control and one status indicator, as specified; no volume/call buttons
  were added. The hub includes the specified rear clip and keyed breakaway.
- The visible contact layout follows v0.3: fin active, separate body return,
  two independent canal EEG contacts, separate concha EEG reference and the
  shared right DRL. Artistic contact counts or placements in the images do
  not override that architecture. The inactive mirrored left DRL remains an
  explicit interpretation of the identical-hardware requirement.
- The source's per-ear PPG, IMU and speaker, and hub electronics allocations,
  remain inspectable even where the reference image does not reveal them.
- Both full-length branches and the full trunk remain modeled. The complete
  system uses a display coil; the Product pair uses display-only posing.

BOUNDARIES
The images do not establish size, fit, wall thickness, a connector design or
an internal layout. Those remain documented design assumptions. Fine braid and finish grain are viewer shaders. GLB transmission uses standard
material extensions where supported. These effects are not textile solid
geometry or measured material properties. Reallocated hub packages are
provisional spatial envelopes, not available-component selections.
