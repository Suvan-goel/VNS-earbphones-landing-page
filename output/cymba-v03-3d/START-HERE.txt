CYMBA / EARBUD COGNITION DEVICE
Specification v0.3 — detailed 3D concept, third visual revision
21 October 2026

WHAT THIS REVISION FOLLOWS
The v0.3 technical specification defines functions and contact architecture.
The locked site reference and active premium-pair hero define appearance:
plain black oval faceplates, front champagne bezels, smoked rear shells,
curled retention horns, smoke-grey tips and a slender hub.
CHANGES-TO-MATCH-IMAGERY.txt explains the retained differences from the images.

OPEN / EXPLORE
Open model-review.html for the self-contained interactive review. The file
embeds its viewer libraries and geometry. Browser/WebGL support and local-file
policies determine whether this viewing mode is available; the modular
index.html version can also be served by a local static web server.

The Product view pairs a three-quarter exterior view with a contact-side view, inward
tips and presentation leads cropped at the stage edge. Choose Left or Right earbud, then
Exterior, Contacts or Profile, to inspect the other surfaces. The hub, complete
system, fit kit and cable section also have individual views. Drag to orbit, scroll to zoom, and use the
construction slider or housing switch to inspect internal components.
Select a surface or an item under Inspect components to read its role, source,
assumptions and unposed envelope. Display posing does not alter source parts.
CAD download links refer to adjacent files; keep the extracted folder intact.

The FreeCAD file is an assembly of editable solid objects, not a fully
constrained sketch/feature-history model. Change the construction parameters
and regenerate to revise the design consistently. The GLB includes material transmission for supporting viewers. Fine braid and
finish-grain shading are added by the interactive viewer; STEP does not store
those effects or individual textile fibres. The cable solid and its full length remain CAD.

THIS REVISION
- Tapered earbud bodies with plain satin-black crowned faceplate inserts and
  visible champagne bezels around the front edge; no earbud branding.
- Rounded, deeply curled retention horns with flush curved active contacts.
- Distinct smoke-grey bell tips, a longer exposed neck and narrower EEG inlays.
- Smoked shell appearance, black silicone fins and warm metallic body returns.
- Slender 25 × 77 × 12 mm nominal hub, with one stop control and one status lens.
- Thinner 3.0 mm trunk and curve-following braid shading in the viewer.

FUNCTIONS PRESERVED
Both earbuds retain the specified stimulation and sensing hardware: separate
fin active contact and body return, two canal EEG contacts, concha EEG reference,
PPG, IMU and audio. The right DRL is shared; the mirrored inactive left pad is
a disclosed interpretation. Each fin has approximately 75.4 mm2 of exposed
curved active contact, and each body return has a 100 mm2 planar face.
The branches target 150 mm exposed centreline each; the trunk targets 1000 mm.
The complete-system trunk is coiled for presentation. The hub retains a clip,
battery and electronics allocations, physical stop, status light, haptic unit,
USB-C and a keyed magnetic breakaway. The cable specimen shows shield groups.

READ THE ASSUMPTIONS / CHECKS
SPECIFICATION-NOTES.txt distinguishes requirements from appearance choices and
provisional dimensions. model-manifest.json supplies per-part metadata.
validation.json, CAD-INTERFERENCE-AUDIT.txt, HUB-VALIDATION.txt and
CABLE-VALIDATION.txt record checks within their stated scopes. Consult the
current generated reports for actual geometry results and unresolved overlaps.

The source supplies no production drawings, final packages or ear-scan data.
This remains a concept and packaging study. Anatomical fit, contact pressure,
tolerances, sealing, manufacture, electrical design and medical performance
remain unvalidated. Electronic blocks are spatial allocations, not selected
components or circuit designs. No stimulation firmware is included.

REGENERATE
Use FreeCAD's Python interpreter with source/build.py, then source/style_cad.py
with QT_QPA_PLATFORM=offscreen for native colours and STEP exports.
Use Python 3 with source/package_viewer.py to rebuild the self-contained review,
and source/package_release.py to rebuild the ZIP and file checksums.
The construction scripts use the installed macOS FreeCAD path; adapt that path
for other platforms. Regeneration does not replace the documented CAD checks.

Viewer library: Three.js r170, MIT licence in vendor/LICENSE.

PORTABLE CABLE DISPLAY
The three GLB cable meshes are capped circular sweeps of their saved CAD
centreline samples, with 32 segments around each cross section. This avoids
a display triangulation sliver in the long swept CAD surface. CAD/STEP solids,
centreline lengths and metadata are unchanged. See CABLE-MESH-VALIDATION.txt.
