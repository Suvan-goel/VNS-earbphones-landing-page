Current neural mannequin
User-supplied Meshy AI neural head bust, supplied 25 October 2026.
Source filename: Meshy_AI_neural_head_bust_fron_1025174930_image-to-3d-texture.glb
Refined in Blender: retained the supplied sculpture, reduced mesh density,
baked surface grain and matte roughness for a honed stone finish,
smoothed the original scalp relief and rebuilt the purple neural network as
connected, tapered fibres across the scalp, crown and back, with continuous
neck routes and a scroll-driven growth reveal. Compressed for web display.
The neural highlights are schematic artistic
projections, not anatomically exact pathways or measured activation.

Current white stone surface
Adapted from "Marble 01" by Rob Tuytel, downloaded from Poly Haven.
Source: https://polyhaven.com/a/marble_01
License: CC0 1.0 Universal, https://polyhaven.com/license
https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/
An interior patch of the downloaded displacement and roughness maps is
adapted and baked to the sculpture's existing UVs. Tile joints, broad colour
variation and grey veins are excluded. The surface uses a uniform white
base colour, fine relief and restrained satin roughness.
