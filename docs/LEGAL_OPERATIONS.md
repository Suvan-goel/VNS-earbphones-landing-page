## Privacy requests

- Log receipt, verification steps, scope, response deadline, action and completion. Use proportionate identity checks; do not request full payment-card details.
- Website analytics withdrawal stops future events and requests deletion for retained authenticated analytics tokens. A minimal tombstone prevents late events recreating that session. Failed requests remain pending for retry; do not describe them as completed.
- Historical analytics without a valid retained token cannot be automatically attributed to a requester. Old campaign information may be attached to waitlist/reservation records. Locate and erase identifiable consent-derived information through a verified manual request where possible; document any identification limits and applicable exceptions.
- Advertising withdrawal revokes Pixel permission and clears accessible browser storage. It does not itself erase data already received by Meta. Handle relevant erasure requests and recipient notifications using the provider's supported process; never promise a third-party deletion without confirmation.
- A browser erasure request is not cancellation of a paid reservation or erasure of legally required accounting records.

## Retention

- At the end of the early-access programme, delete or anonymise remaining first-party analytics and remove expired launch-list/survey/Meta lead information in accordance with the notice.
- Review support correspondence once resolved. Keep only information needed for follow-up, outstanding benefits, legal obligations or active disputes.
- Keep referral records while rewards remain outstanding and to document fulfilment. Apply transaction retention requirements to payment/refund evidence.
- Record applicable accounting, tax and legal-claim retention periods for the actual business and markets. Do not invent a universal number of years.
- The application does not install a scheduled purge in this update. End-of-programme deletion and legacy requests require an operational process.
